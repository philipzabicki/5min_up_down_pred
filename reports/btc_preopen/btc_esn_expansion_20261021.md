The previous ranges below refer to the completed R4 development study. Observed ranges are descriptive of its actual configurations, not new selection criteria. ## Limits and next-step guidance

Dense recurrence was capped at 256 total units and orthogonal recurrence at 512. The benchmark measured 2,048-unit deep and parallel_deep state generation at 32.85–34.03s and 17.14–17.62s per 43,200-row fragment; extrapolating to the 2,660,546-row cache and three finalist seeds gives about 105 and 54 minutes for those candidates alone. Both multi-layer families are therefore capped at 1,024 units to protect the 3-hour finalist/portfolio reserve. Benchmark-only 2,048-unit deep variants are not search results.
- Read boundary diagnostics by probe, downstream, family, and total-unit budget. Do not infer a family advantage from its single best candidate.
- This search does not establish an ESN advantage outside the explored configurations or replace independent confirmation.
