# Historical BTC Polymarket contract

## Timing, established before implementation

`Opened` is the opening time of a CLOSED Binance index one-minute candle (naive artifacts mean UTC). Its OHLC and features are available no earlier than `Opened + 1 minute`. Live `_predict_next_bucket` consumes that candle, prepares features, predicts, then evaluates the quotes. Only minute % 5 == 4 rows are live decision rows; market start is `Opened + 1 minute`, end is start + 5 minutes. The proxy compares Close at Opened with Close at Opened + 5 minutes (ties UP). Target weights emphasize these decision rows; other minutes are NOT extra live entries.

Historical wall-clock receive/inference times are unavailable. Scenarios 0, 1, 2 seconds are explicit lower-bound / latency assumptions, not measured live availability. `decision_available_at = Opened + 1 minute + scenario latency`. Select the first recorded quote AT OR AFTER this instant, within an explicit delay limit and strictly before expiry. Never nearest, interpolation, or forward fill. Quote delay is measured from decision_available_at. Kacho samples a cached book at 1 Hz: the original exchange book age and actual fill cannot be proven. Age is unknown, never reported as fresh.

## Data layers

Raw immutable revision-pinned Hugging Face files and official Gamma responses reside under data/raw/polymarket. Manifests contain SHA256, size, revision, download time and inspected coverage/counts. Each run discovers the currently published source revisions and all orderbook partitions. Every partition is downloaded resumably, checksum-verified and inspected in batches for actual BTC 5m coverage, including empty partitions. Trades, mid-price histories and underlying prices do not supply executable ask/size pairs and are not substitutes for missing books.

Canonical market/quote Parquet and decision datasets reside under data/datasets/polymarket/BTC/runs/<fingerprint>, reports under data/analysis/polymarket/BTC/runs/<fingerprint>. The fingerprint includes the OOF file content hash, model metadata, revisions, pipeline code, live configuration, latencies and quote tolerance. Probability-dependent outputs are always rebuilt; raw files and verified settlement caches are retained. Root evaluation.json points to the current run and is invalidated before execution starts; old benchmark reports/artifacts are not active inputs.

Kacho outcome is a final-bid inference: retain it ONLY for audit, never settlement truth. Official resolved metadata supplies settlement, token order, boundaries and historical per-market feeSchedule. Gamma startDate is listing time; use eventStartTime / events.startTime and slug epoch for trading-window start. Unknown or mismatched identities/boundaries are excluded. Official response cache includes fetch time; unresolved cached markets require explicit cache removal to refresh.

Kacho records ASK top size and BID aggregate depth only. Top-size-constrained ask execution is possible; deeper ASK VWAP is unavailable. Aggregate bid depth cannot finance BUY execution. Counterfactual buckets are unavailable when ask size is insufficient. Fees reuse utils.polymarket and net shares follow live build_trade_intent: (gross stake - fee) / ask. Stakes include fees.

Obadiaha retains long per-token snapshots and numeric L2 levels. UP/DOWN identity comes from independently verified official metadata. Choose the first forward snapshot separately for each side, both within the same 2s signal tolerance and before expiry. Preserve both capture times and their gap; execute at the later capture. This asynchronous snapshot assumption does not prove simultaneously executable sides or guaranteed fills. Missing, crossed, inconsistent top levels, unknown tokens and duplicated/nonmonotonic token captures are excluded. Only observed top ask size finances a BUY; no deeper guaranteed fill is assumed.

The raw Obadiaha arrays retain at most ten levels, predominantly bids ascending from 0.01 and asks descending from 0.99. Reported best prices equal the extrema of these stored levels, not a verified full-book top. The adapter sorts stored levels correctly and records `best_prices_scope=stored_levels_only_full_book_unknown`. Missing better levels cannot be reconstructed. Existing stored-price economics are retained as a reference; neither ask=0.90 nor zero trades establishes the true contemporaneous spread or model quality. Full responses or collector code are required to distinguish actual wide books from truncation conclusively.

Providers are validated independently, even without overlap. For common markets verify condition ID, UP/DOWN token IDs, start/end and official outcome/availability time; conflicting metadata blocks union. Select eligible Kacho before eligible Obadiaha by fixed source priority independent of PnL, then keep at most one decision/trade per market per latency. Provider provenance remains on decisions and trade ledgers. coverage.json gives actual ranges, per-partition BTC counts, OOF overlap, gaps and exclusion reasons for every source/scenario.

Combined evaluation and independent per-source evaluations reuse the same walk-forward and portfolio functions. Earlier source periods can supply only warmup observations in the combined chronology; independent evaluation also measures their economics. Every source/policy/latency comparison owns a separate continuous $100 portfolio. Never sum source portfolios or latency scenarios. Evaluation-source coverage distinguishes valid data used for warmup from data actually evaluated economically.

## Prediction provenance and evaluation

Resolve the current BTC OOF through load_modeling_settings(asset='BTC') and resolve_oof_prediction_output_paths. The input column is oof_pred_proba_up, mapped to p_model_up. Validate unique UTC minute timestamps and finite probabilities in [0,1]; require exact market-start matching to Opened+1m, never a later prediction, nearest matching or filling coverage gaps. Audit runtime model metadata, file modification time, SHA256, rows, probabilities, timestamp range, training coverage and decision-grid coverage in oof_manifest.json. A missing artifact fails with its exact absolute path.

The user accepts the main-model OOF generation, including evaluation-fold early stopping, as an experiment assumption. Missing row-level historical fold evidence is not a blocker. The historical pipeline does not retrain the source BTC model, change modeling/feature/indicator/volume-profile settings, read final-model predictions, or fall back to the removed ten-feature benchmark. Settlement calibration/correction may train over its unchanged OOF. This is a retrospective economic evaluation of the supplied OOF; it does not establish prospective independence of the original model selection.

Probability calibration uses official outcome and only labels settled before the outer fold starts. Policy choice uses a separate later historical validation block after inner calibration training; evaluation is the untouched next chronological block. The first 40% supplies initial past observations; the remaining 60% is evaluated in three folds. Calibration and policy choice for each fold see only its earlier available outcomes. Fixed baseline policies and the past-selected policy strategy are reported separately. Every baseline and selector owns one continuous $100 portfolio per latency scenario. Concatenating chronological evaluation blocks before simulation preserves cash, positions, locked stake and original settlement conditions across policy/calibrator changes; fold bankrolls are never reset or summed as the headline.

Orders debit gross stake including fees from available cash only. Settlement payout becomes available at max(official resolved_at_utc, market_end_utc), with zero additional redemption delay as an explicit assumption. No deposits or borrowing are allowed. Minimum-order unaffordability is recorded without stopping pending settlements. After all positions settle, final balance equals $100 plus summed trade PnL and summed cash flows; PnL cannot be below -$100.

Bankroll paths distinguish cash, open_position_cost, realized_pnl and cash_flow. open_position_market_value and mark_to_market_equity are unavailable (null) while positions are open, rather than being set to zero. cost_basis_equity (and compatibility bankroll) equals cash+open_position_cost; its drawdown is explicitly cost basis, not mark-to-market. Trade ledgers record the probability actually used: raw p_model_up for current live entry EV/sizing, p_calibrated for the other policies. Runtime configuration and shared run.py entry/sizing functions are reused. Early exits, actual FAK fills and redemption/withdrawal behavior cannot be reconstructed and are documented limits.

Architecture: long-history predictive modeling -> a separate retrospective settlement-prediction comparison -> economic entry and sizing analysis. The source BTC model/OOF and live policy remain unchanged. Earlier settlement calibration and loss-attribution experiments do not consume Polymarket prices as probability features; the dedicated market-value experiment below explicitly tests a second-layer model that does.

## Loss diagnosis and retrospective adaptation

`EXPERIMENT_MODE='loss_diagnosis'` selects the earlier loss investigation, configured as a file constant. It resolves the last complete evaluation manifest, verifies raw-file checksums, current OOF/model metadata, feature subset, modeling settings and protected live code/configuration, and reproduces all original portfolios before research. Original input/output directories and evaluation.json remain intact. Research has a separate fingerprinted run under the existing analysis pipeline and a loss_diagnosis.json pointer. Its signature includes parent inputs, raw manifests, official-cache manifests, canonical decisions, original calibrated folds, code and research constants. `rebuild_history` retains original ingestion behavior.

The default experiment is `EXPERIMENT_MODE='market_value'`. It compares Kacho market-only logistic models against identical models augmented with the unchanged source OOF, plus an optional closed-candle context extension. It retains only condition IDs with valid quotes and all four finite nonnegative best-level sizes in each 0/1/2-second source-latency dataset, so the market sample is paired. The observation is the first forward Kacho tick available to the source OOF scenario, within the existing 2-second quote bound. It is used as the second-layer feature timestamp; zero additional computation delay is an explicit optimistic assumption. Book prices/sizes are model inputs only in this dedicated experiment. Historical labels must be officially available before fitting or tuning. Three chronological evaluation folds use the first 40% as initial history, with identical L2 logistic families, scaling, candidate strengths, and inner validation budget for market-only and OOF-augmented models. Results include paired three-day block uncertainty, probability calibration, fixed-$5 fee-inclusive decisions, delayed execution at 0/1/2 seconds, and one continuous $100 portfolio per latency/model/delay. For delayed execution, side and maximum ask are frozen from the feature-time quote; the later quote can reject the order but cannot change its side or limit. The fingerprinted market-value run and report preserve all earlier diagnosis artifacts. Obadiaha is excluded because its captured stored levels do not establish executable full-book top prices. Neither this experiment nor its outputs change live code or configuration.

Attribution A/B/C uses identical executed entries, sides and gross stakes. A uses official labels/net shares, B uses official labels and fee-free shares at the same recorded ask, and C replaces only the official label with the Binance proxy. B/C never reselect orders; C is a label diagnostic, not an executable Polymarket backtest. Fee-free share demand exceeding observed size is counted explicitly. Expected PnL is purchased-side probability × net shares − gross stake. DOWN uses 1−p_UP. Entry edge before a buffer, edge after a buffer, expected PnL and realized PnL are distinct quantities.

Independent fixed-stake signal diagnostics reuse entry/minimum-order/observed-liquidity rules through the full evaluated period and ignore portfolio cash exhaustion. They have no interpretable $100 final balance or drawdown; aggregate PnL/turnover is reported separately. Ordinary portfolios still never borrow, receive deposits or reset between folds. Decision reasons distinguish no edge, insufficient cash, minimum order, locked capital, liquidity and policy abstention; stopping below minimum is not evidence of effective risk control.

Research compares raw OOF, existing past-selected one-dimensional calibration and a penalized residual correction with mandatory raw-logit offset. Only last closed-minute log return and trailing 30-minute volatility enter context, including a logit×volatility interaction. Scaling and regularization fit earlier data only. Missing minutes invalidate context rather than turning multi-minute moves into one-minute returns. Polymarket quotes, future movement, final target mismatch and settlement never enter the correction.

Outer folds use only earlier labels available strictly before their start at max(official resolution, expiry). Inner training precedes selection. Regularization, calibrator and observed-spread margin choices use the earlier inner block; evaluated blocks never tune them. The previously studied period is called retrospective walk-forward, not an untouched holdout. Latency comparisons use the intersection of eligible market IDs; native and common source/period coverage are reported separately. Probability/sizing combinations share evaluation moments, and frozen raw-entry diagnostics isolate probability quality from reselection.

Uncertainty uses paired three-day moving-block bootstrap for probability-score differences and three-day blocks of executed-bet PnL/turnover including zero-trade days. These intervals condition on observed bets and do not reselect policies or simulate portfolio survival. Time-block summaries, counts, purchased-side calibration, raw-source evidence and all rejection reasons accompany headline balances. Live settings and run.py are untouched; exact-fee entry and spread margins are research variants only.
